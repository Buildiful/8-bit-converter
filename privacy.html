Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Buildiful® offers this free tool whether or not you allow optional website analytics.

Your images stay on your device

The converter reads, transforms, previews and downloads your selected image in your browser. The converter does not upload your images, filenames, pixel data or generated PNG files to Buildiful, Google or another service. It does not use a server for image processing. The image data remains on your device while you use the page.

Website analytics

On the official GitHub Pages site, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), when enabled, to understand page visits and basic tool usage, including successful image conversions and initiated PNG downloads. We use these measurements to understand and improve our free tools. Our custom analytics events do not contain selected images, filenames, image dimensions, image data, account identifiers or document contents. We configure the tag to omit URL query parameters and fragments and to disable Google Signals and advertising personalization in its tag settings. Advertising-related consent defaults are set to denied.

When GA4 is enabled, Google may receive technical information about your visit, such as browser or device information and network information used to derive an approximate location. GA4 may use first-party cookies, including _ga cookies containing pseudonymous identifiers, to distinguish visitors and sessions. Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. For further details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and GA4 cookie information.

Your choice

Visitors detected in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland or Quebec are asked to opt in before GA4 loads. In other detected regions, GA4 may be enabled without a prior prompt, but you can decline it through the Privacy control in the converter footer. If we cannot determine the region, GA4 stays off until you choose to allow it. A browser Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal also prevents the app from enabling GA4.

To review or change your choice, return to 8-Bit Converter, select Privacy in the footer, then choose No thanks or Allow analytics. The dialog also links back to this notice. Declining analytics prevents GA4 from loading on a new page visit; if it has already loaded during the current visit, the app disables further GA4 measurement and updates the consent state. Previously collected information is not automatically removed from Google's systems, and cookies previously set by GA4 may remain until they expire or you clear them in your browser. Disabling analytics does not affect the converter.

Approximate region lookup

To select the appropriate consent experience, the app may request country and province information from IPWhois using https://ipwho.is/. The provider receives a normal network request that includes your IP address and may process the request under its privacy policy. The app saves only a Boolean result indicating whether an opt-in prompt is required, for up to 12 hours in your browser. It does not save the returned IP address, city or coordinates. The lookup is separate from Google Analytics and is not performed when your browser sends a supported privacy signal or when a valid analytics choice is already saved.

Local settings and retention

Your explicit analytics choice and its timestamp are saved in first-party browser local storage for up to 365 days. Because compatible Buildiful tools use the same buildiful.github.io origin, they may share this setting. Clearing browser storage can reset the choice. Region classification is cached for up to 12 hours. Neither setting contains your image.

When GA4 is enabled, Google processes and retains measurement data under the GA4 property's configuration and Google's service policies. Standard GA4 properties permit user-level and event-level retention settings of 2 or 14 months; the actual configured period cannot be determined from this web page. Standard aggregated reports may remain available beyond those event-level retention periods. Contact Buildiful for the current property-specific setting or a data-related inquiry. See Google's data retention information.

Other services and contact

This site is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub and the network providers delivering the site may independently process ordinary web requests, regardless of your GA4 choice. This notice describes the converter and its analytics configuration; it does not cover every practice of those external providers.

For questions about privacy, applicable access, objection or deletion rights, or other data-related requests, please use the contact options on Buildiful's official website. You can also consult the privacy information of the relevant third-party providers linked above.
